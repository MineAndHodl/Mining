Desk Score blends edge, expected value, and role certainty — a moderate edge on a locked-in role outranks a huge edge on an unknown (a blowout gap usually means our number's stale, not that we found free money). Best-across shows the top plays regardless of category, so a weak category can contribute nothing. Two parlay lanes: the Whale Missile is 8-10 near-locks across different games (independent, so a lot of them hit), while each Primetime SGP is 8-10 legs in the same game, correlated so they hit together in a shootout.
