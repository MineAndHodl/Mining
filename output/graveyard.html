Method, because receipts: every test runs against real closing lines (nflverse, 2015–2025), walk-forward where models are involved, with a Bonferroni-corrected significance bar for the number of angles tested (15 simultaneous tests = p < 0.0033 to survive). An angle that "hits 54%" on a cherry-picked sample is how touts are born; an angle that clears a corrected bar out-of-sample is how bets are made. Almost nothing clears the bar. That is the single most important fact in sports betting.
