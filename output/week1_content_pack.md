## The optics layer (from wavey's live feed)

These run alongside the sharp content. Everything gets logged at real, timestamped prices — that constraint is what lets the optics survive scrutiny.

**The Banker (win-rate engine).** One heavy favorite per day (-180 to -300 range, model-approved). Marketed as a streak: "Banker: 11-1 in its last 12." Win percentage is the marketing stat; the record page grades it in units so the ROI stays truthful. This is the honest version of "minus money that always wins" — short-price streaks are real and sellable, they're just not where profit comes from, and we never pretend otherwise on the record page.

**The Ghost Number (the Caruso effect, done clean).** Our off-market flags are literally numbers that vanish — that's what a stale line is. Every flag gets posted with book, price, and timestamp. When it moves: "Posted MIA +3.5 at 10:02am. It's +1.5 everywhere by noon. This is why the card drops Tuesday — paid gets it the second it fires." The urgency is real, the receipts are checkable, and it converts exactly the FOMO Caruso manufactures. What we don't do is grade ourselves at prices followers never had — that's the #1 thing that gets cappers publicly burned in their own Whop reviews, and one screenshot thread ends the brand.

**Live sweats (game windows).** 2-3 scheduled in-game posts per slate: "one leg down 👀", GIF reactions, the cash-out tease. Semi-automatable (score-triggered), best with your thumb on it Sunday.

**Giveaways tied to game events.** "$100 to a random RT when [our top play] covers." Ties virality directly to the pick, pays only on wins, costs $100. Needs you to fund and pick winners (raffles have platform rules — keep it small and manual early).

**Pivot boards (Discord retention).** The dense daily prop menu with pivots — this is what makes paid feel alive daily. NFL version ships with the props lab in phase 2; until then Thursday/Sunday slate boards fill the slot.

**🐋🚀 THE WHALE MISSILE (primetime lotto SGPs — one per TNF/SNF/MNF).** The flagship degen product. One massive same-game parlay per primetime game, built from the model's actual game-script read: the spread/total lean picks the script, the script picks the stack (trailing team's QB attempts + WR1 receptions + game total, or a control-script RB stack + under, etc.). Legs are correlated on purpose — correlated stacks hit together more often than the multiplied price implies, which is the only honest way to build lotto slips. Small stake, clearly labeled a lottery play, logged on the record page with everything else.

Drop cadence: Thursday 4pm (TNF missile), Sunday 4pm (SNF missile), Monday 4pm (MNF missile). Drop copy template:
> 🐋🚀 TONIGHT'S WHALE MISSILE
> [slip graphic — legs + combined odds]
> One per primetime game. Small unit. When it hits you'll hear about it for a month. Full construction logic in the Discord.

When one hits: pin it, quote it all week, giveaway on the RT. When it misses: "missile down. they can't all land. [record page] tracks every launch." The season-long "missile log" (all launches, hits, net units) is itself a receipt post — no other account grades their lotto slips.

Naming: "Whale Missile" is the public brand (group-chat test: "you see the whale missile tonight?" — passes). The full ceremonial name — Nuclear Whale Missile Piss Play — lives in the Discord as the community in-joke and the #nwmpp channel name; inside jokes that members feel ownership of are retention glue. Alt names if you want options: The Nuke, Fat Slip Friday, The Harpoon. Recommendation: Whale Missile public, NWMPP internal.

**Loss spin bank (rotate, never hide).** "I gotta do better than this 😏" self-deprecation · "ball don't lie, number was right" CLV frame · "on the bright side ⏳" pivot to next slate · hobby/community frame: "we eat together, we take Ls together." Spin is voice, not accounting — the L posts to the record page the same hour either way.
