The Desk

We don't out-predict the market. We beat the close. The Desk posts a number early — while the book's number is still soft — and you're already up by kickoff.
Every pick states three things: our projection (the Desk number), the line we'll play it up to / down to, and the best number on the board right now. No number, no pick.

How the edge actually works

Model sets our number→ Market opens soft→ We post it Tuesday, early→ Line moves toward our number→ You already beat the close
The honest core finding, from 2,335 backtested games: no public model beats the NFL closing line outright (~48–50% ATS). So we don't try to. The winnable game is open→close: openers are soft, and if we can predict which way a number drifts, betting it early is positive expected CLV even when we can't beat the close itself. Cross-book shopping is a secondary confirmation, not the product — most people don't have six apps open.
