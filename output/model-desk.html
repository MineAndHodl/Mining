Build report · Sept 1, 2026

NFL Model Desk

EPA power-rating engine, walk-forward backtest 2017–2025, opening 2026 board, and the operating plan for the season.

01 What got built

Team offense/defense ratings from play-by-play EPA (2013–2025, ~460k plays): pass-weighted composite (62/38), opponent-adjusted iteratively, exponentially recency-weighted (10-week halflife), shrunk to league mean, with offseason staleness at each season boundary. Ratings feed three projection layers — spread (rating gap + home field 1.7 + rest), total (combined off/def efficiency + dome + wind against the current scoring environment), and moneyline (projected margin → win probability, σ = 13.2). Every backtest number below is walk-forward: each week's projection uses only games played before that week, and each season's coefficients are fit only on prior seasons.

Breakeven at -110 is 52.4%. The model never gets there. The sharper test: regressing actual margins on the closing line and the model number, the model's coefficient is zero (p = 0.35; totals p = 0.62) — the closing line already contains everything the model knows, plus injuries, weather forecasts, and sharp money it can't see. Model error vs actual margin: 10.30 points; the market's: 9.86.

The finding, plainly: no model built on public data beats NFL closing lines. Not this one, not the paid ones. Any handicapper claiming 55%+ against the close is selling variance. That's not the end of the project — it defines where the real edge is.

03 Where the alpha actually is

Off-market numbers. The closing consensus is efficient; individual books at individual moments are not. The pipeline de-vigs Pinnacle (the sharp anchor) and flags any retail book whose spread or total sits 1+ point off the fair number — 0.5 when it crosses 3 or 7 — or whose moneyline price clears +2% EV against fair probability. Betting numbers that already beat the sharp price is positive expected CLV by construction. This is how winning retail bettors actually win, and it's fully automatable with a live odds feed.

Early-week lines. Tuesday openers are softer than Sunday closers. The Tuesday card catches numbers before the market finishes correcting; the Sunday pass catches late off-market moves and injury repricing.

Key numbers. Landing the better side of 3 and 7 is worth roughly 2 points of win probability per crossing. The flagger treats key-number crossings as bets even at half the normal threshold.

Props (phase 2). Player prop markets carry more vig but far less sharp attention. Same architecture — usage-based projections vs cross-book outliers — once the core loop is running.

The model's real job is context, not picks: an independent number to judge which off-market flags are real, a sanity check when news breaks, and a prior in thin early-season markets. One tested overlay worth knowing: 10+ mph wind games went under at 58% from 2017–2023 — then 45% in 2024 and 53% in 2025. The market patched it. That's the pattern with published angles, and why the desk anchors on prices, not narratives.

Reading the gaps honestly: the model carries zero 2026 offseason information, so the very largest gaps (ARI, DAL, CIN) are more likely measuring roster news than market error — those get diagnosed, not bet. Futures also lock capital for five months and carry heavy vig — small units, and only where the gap survives the offseason-news check.

06 Operating plan

Tuesday, 10am — card drops: off-market flags vs Pinnacle fair across every book on the feed, key-number opportunities, model deltas as context, injury/news notes. You review, push back, approve.

Sunday, 9am — final pass: line moves since Tuesday, late off-market numbers, inactives.

Monday — grading: every flagged number logged with the price taken and the closing price. CLV is the KPI, not weekly W-L — beating the close consistently is the only early signal that generalizes, and 30 bets of results is noise. Verdict on whether the desk is +EV comes at ~100 tracked bets; if CLV is flat-to-negative by then, we change the approach rather than the story.

To go live, one thing: a free key from the-odds-api.com — sign up, key arrives by email, paste it in chat. 500 requests/month covers the Tue/Sun cadence with room to spare and unlocks the whole loop: multi-book spreads, totals, and moneylines including Pinnacle.

All backtest code is walk-forward with no leakage; results reproducible in the session workspace. Sizing decisions stay with you — the desk flags numbers and tracks whether they beat the close. Variance in this market is brutal even with real CLV; judge the operation on the tracked record, not any single week.
